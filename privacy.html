CLEAR ROAD / G1

Privacy policy

Effective 25 October 2026. This policy describes the current Clear Road G1 iPhone app.

Study information

Your plan, lesson completion, answers, saved questions, and review history are stored on your iPhone. The app does not send this study information to our servers, use it for ads, or sell it. Uninstalling the app removes this on-device study data. There is no in-app account or cloud study-progress sync in this version.

Purchases

Apple processes in-app payments. The app reads Apple-verified purchase transactions, including product, date and revocation status, to determine access. We do not receive your card number. Apple’s handling of payment and Apple Account information is governed by Apple’s policies.

External links and support

Opening a handbook, DriveTest, refund, or support link leaves the app. Those services handle information under their own policies. A support request you choose to post through GitHub is handled by GitHub and may be public; do not post sensitive personal information.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions, contact us through support. We will update this page if the app’s data practices change.
