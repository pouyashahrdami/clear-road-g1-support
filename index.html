CLEAR ROAD / G1

Support

Clear Road G1 is an independent Ontario G1 study app. If something in the app is unclear, a purchase is not available, or a question looks wrong, please open a support request. Include the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and the question ID if relevant. Do not include payment details or sensitive personal information.

Purchases

Use Restore purchases in Settings or on the access screen with the Apple Account used to buy a pass. Apple handles charges and refund requests. To request a refund, use Apple’s refund page.

Official study source

Check current driving rules in the Official MTO Driver’s Handbook. Clear Road is not affiliated with the Government of Ontario or DriveTest.
